# Vestris surface notes (measured)

Source: https://vestris.ai, captured at 1440×900 on 2026-10-18.

## Palette

| Sample | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Page background | `rgb(242, 239, 229)` ≈ `#f2efe5` | cream canvas |
| Primary button | `rgb(98, 101, 78)` ≈ `#62654e` | olive pill, cream text |
| Secondary button | `rgb(242, 239, 229)` on cream | “Sign in” pill, subtle border |
| Body ink | near `#1a1a17` | headings, nav |
| Secondary copy | warm gray ≈ `#4b4b43` | hero paragraph |
| Illustration | soft sky blue + sage green + warm white | botanical collage |
| Accent chip | YC orange `#f26625` | only small proof chips |

The page is almost entirely cream + olive + ink. There is no purple, no blue UI chrome, no saturated gradient CTA.

## Typography

- Display: **Instrument Serif** (computed fonts list), 400, mixed upright + italic:
  `The file *moves itself*` at roughly 110px, line-height ~1, letter-spacing tight.
- Body/nav: humanist sans (Geist / Manrope / Inter loaded). Nav labels ~15px, 400.
- Hero paragraph: ~24px, line-height 1.4, centered, max-width ~700px.
- No bold display; contrast comes from size and the italic word.

## Navigation (the signature)

- Floating cream band at top, roughly 90px tall, inner content max ~1430 but visually inset.
- Left: hand-drawn leaf mark + serif wordmark “Vestris” (~30px).
- Center: 3 plain links (Product / Pricing / Vision), 15px, no boxes.
- Right: “Sign in” in a cream capsule (46px tall, radius 8, light border) + “Show Demo” in olive capsule (46px, radius 8, cream text).
- The nav container is translucent/soft against the cream page and uses generous horizontal spacing; it is not a full-width white bar.
- The area behind the nav has a very subtle lighter cream halo, creating a floating panel feel.

## Components

- Buttons: radius 8px, padding 14–19px vertical / 19–24px horizontal, no loud shadows.
- Hero CTA: 58px tall, olive, cream text, radius 8.
- App preview: large rounded browser-like panel (top radius ~20px), soft shadow, floating over an illustrated sky/sage band. It reads as a real product screenshot, not a decorative mockup.
- Section transitions: hard photographic/illustrated bands, not card walls.
- Content is centered on the marketing page, but that centered layout is **not** the model for our product pages.

## What to borrow

1. Cream/olive/ink palette and the calm, paper-like feel.
2. Instrument Serif with one italic phrase in display lines.
3. Floating translucent nav with quiet secondary pill + olive primary pill.
4. 8px buttons, 16–24px media frames, soft large shadows.
5. Product previews built from real content over a soft organic gradient field.

## What not to borrow

- The centered marketing hero composition.
- The photographic botanical collage or any stock illustration.
- The one-page marketing structure.
- Its exact wordmark/leaf asset.
