# Reference notes

Measured at 1440×900; use as starting points, not pixel laws.

## Stripe — layout

- White header ~76px; content shell ~1232px; left-aligned hero.
- Hero heading 48px / 55px line-height, weight ~300; eyebrow above.
- Primary button 8px radius; secondary is white with a 1px border.
- Hairline logo strip; 2/3-column feature cards with real product UI; bordered panels; numbered steps; dark multi-column footer.
- Generous whitespace, thin rules, subtle reveal and hover motion.

**Borrow:** left alignment, 12-column rhythm, hairline sections, product previews, card grids, numbered steps, one dark closing block.

**Do not borrow:** purple/blue palette, gradient ribbon art, fake logo wall, centered one-page marketing narrative.

## Vestris — surface

- Page `#f2efe5`; primary olive `#62654e`; near-black ink; botanical sky/sage accents.
- Instrument Serif display, mixed upright + italic; humanist sans UI.
- Floating cream nav: leaf mark + serif wordmark left, 3 plain links center, cream sign-in pill + olive primary pill right; 46px buttons, 8px radius.
- Hero CTA 58px tall, 8px radius, cream text.
- Product screenshot in a large rounded frame (top radius ~20px) over an illustrated gradient band; quiet shadows.

**Borrow:** cream/olive palette, serif italic accent, floating nav, 8–24px radii, soft product frames.

**Do not borrow:** centered marketing composition, botanical photo collage, exact wordmark/assets, one-page structure.

## Translation

1. Stripe's **structure** on a Vestris **surface**.
2. Left-align product pages; reserve centering for auth/empty states.
3. Keep 1px rules and generous padding; use shadows only for interactive/hover surfaces.
4. Show real content in previews; never decorative fake dashboards.
5. One editorial display moment per page; everything else stays quiet.
6. Test the font pairing in the target language before committing to it.
